OUR PEACE PATH

Six ways to grow together 🌼

Explore each theme. Learn useful English words and try a little action every day!

THEME 1

💗 Kindness and Respect

Use kind words and show respect to everyone.

Words: kind, polite, friendly, respect

Try: “Please”, “Thank you”, “You are welcome.”

THEME 2

😊 Emotions and Empathy

Name your feelings and care about other people's feelings.

Words: happy, sad, angry, worried

Try: “How do you feel?” “I understand.”

THEME 3

🕊️ Peaceful Solutions

Listen, talk and ask a trusted adult for help when needed.

Words: listen, talk, solution, apologize

Try: “Let's talk calmly.”

THEME 4

🌈 Inclusion and Diversity

Celebrate what makes each person special.

Words: different, unique, equal, welcome

Try: “Everyone belongs!”

THEME 5

🧩 Teamwork and Helping

Share, cooperate and help your team.

Words: team, share, help, together

Try: “Can I help you?”

THEME 6

🌍 Peace in My Community

Make small positive changes at school and at home.

Words: community, care, peace, friendship

Try: “I can make a difference!”

MORE WAYS TO LEARN

Our Peace Activity Lab 🧪

Fresh classroom ideas to practise English while building a kinder school.

1

🔎 Kindness Detectives

How to play: In pairs, find three examples of kindness in a picture or classroom story.

Say: “I can see kindness because…”

2

🎭 Emotion Charades

Pick an emotion card and act it out without speaking. Friends guess and name a helpful response.

Say: “You look worried. Can I help?”

3

💌 Compliment Pass

Pass a soft ball. Each student gives a respectful compliment to the next person.

Say: “You are a good teammate.”

4

🖍️ Peace Comic Strip

Draw a 3-frame comic: a problem, a safe response and a positive ending. Add speech bubbles in English.

Say: “Please stop. I don't like that.”

5

🗺️ My Peaceful School Map

Draw a school map and mark places where people can feel safe, included and supported.

Say: “The library is a quiet place.”

6

👂 Listening Mission

In pairs, one student speaks for 30 seconds about a hobby. The partner listens and shares one thing they heard.

Say: “I heard that you like…”

7

🌉 Build a Friendship Bridge

Teams build a paper bridge. Each paper strip has one action that helps people work together.

Say: “We can share ideas.”

8

🌱 5-Day Kindness Garden

Each day, add a flower to a class garden after practising one kind action. Celebrate effort, not popularity.

Say: “Today I helped by…”

9

📚 Peace Story Builders

Create a group story. Each student adds one sentence about friendship, courage or asking for help.

Say: “Then, a friend asked a teacher for help.”
